## Brand-usage notes

**Rowan University** (https://www.rowan.edu/marketing-communication/tools-resources/brand-and-visual-identity/graphic-standards/logo-guidelines/)
- Clear area around the logo: at least the radius of the circle.
- Minimum width: 4 picas (2/3 inch); the words "Rowan University" must be legible.
- Use only the supplied variations; do not rearrange art and type or recolor beyond the brand colors; do not print over detailed or high-contrast artwork.
- The logo should identify, not dominate: the title is the primary message.
- Official print files (EPS/PDF) are available on request via the Brand & Marketing Strategy Wrike form; the web SVG above is vector and adequate for print.

**University of Copenhagen** (https://www.ku.dk/designguide/logo)
- "The logo is protected by European trademark registration and may only be used with permission from the University of Copenhagen."
- Use on this poster was confirmed by the co-author (2026-10-09). The poster uses the digital logo (ucph.svg), the standard seal + name line.
- If the logo is printed smaller than 15 mm high, the guide recommends the mini-logo (Minilogo.zip on the same page).
- Co-author affiliation: Department of Computer Science (Faculty of SCIENCE); the faculty print logos are in KU-logo-til-print.zip on the same page if a faculty lock-up is wanted.

**IKLab** (https://iklab.ai)
- No brand or press page was found on iklab.ai. The only logo file is the site-header PNG above; no SVG is published. At 1949 px wide it prints at about 300 dpi up to 6.5 inches wide.
